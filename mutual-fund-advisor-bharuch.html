Why a local advisor, when direct plans are cheaper?

It's a fair question — and one every serious investor should ask. Direct plans skip the distributor commission and cost roughly 1% less per year. If you have the time, the temperament and the training to build a low-overlap portfolio and hold it through every crash without flinching, direct is the right call, and we'll say so.

Most people don't. The 1% you save on a direct plan is small next to the 20–30% of returns investors routinely lose to their own behaviour — panic-selling in a fall, chasing last year's top fund, stopping a SIP at exactly the wrong moment. What you're paying a good advisor for isn't fund-picking. It's the discipline, the planning and the person who picks up the phone in March 2020 and tells you not to sell. That's the value, and we're upfront that we earn distributor commission on the regular plans we transact for you.
